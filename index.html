About us · เกี่ยวกับเรา

A special meal doesn't need a special occasion.

Steak Ban Hao on Kasikorn Thung Sang Road is a neighbourhood steakhouse made for easy, generous meals. Choose your steak (pork neck, chicken, fish or beef), add pasta or a salad, and settle into our cool, comfortable dining room.
